Field report filed by the farmer, for a demonstration of HarvestShield. This is the farmer's own document, not an official record.

Farm: maize, outer Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.
Period: 1 to 14 August 2026.

From 3 August there was no meaningful rain on the farm for several days. By 7 August the maize leaves were rolling by late morning and the lower leaves had started to grey. The topsoil between the rows was dry and cracked by 9 August. Two short showers on 8 and 10 August wet the surface only; the soil was dry again by the next afternoon. Plants tasselling in this period were visibly shorter than in the previous season.

The farmer asks that the dataset whose daily pattern matches these field conditions govern the claim.
